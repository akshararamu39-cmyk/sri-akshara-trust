Curriculum Framework

Progressive Learning Across 4 Stages

Our multi-tier learning trajectory complies with modern national educational guidelines while instilling critical thinking and character.

Foundational Stage

Pre-K, LKG, UKG & Grade 1-2

Play-way, sensory learning, phonetic literacy, numeracy basics, and emotional wellness in vibrant child-friendly activity areas.

Preparatory Stage

Grades 3 to 5

Transitioning to structured conceptual learning, interactive science experiments, reading fluency, and bilingual proficiency.

Middle School

Grades 6 to 8

In-depth math, physics, chemistry, biology, social sciences, computer science coding, and project-based problem solving.

Secondary & Beyond

Grades 9 to 12

Rigorous board preparation, dedicated competitive coaching tracks (JEE, NEET, CUET), career counseling, and leadership seminars.
